# Ethics Statement

Ethics should be about the small design choices engineers make every day. I don't think machines can be truly "ethical" on their own, but I do think we can build them to be less harmful. For me, the most practical takeaway is that transparency matters more than perfection. If a system makes a mistake, people deserve to understand why. Users should be able to understand why a system made a particular decision, especially when that decision affects them. If I cannot explain a design choice to someone who might be hurt by it, I probably should not make that choice.
